Cookie Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy explains the use of cookies and similar technologies on the public corporate website at oraculumlabs.eu.

1. Current cookie use

As of 13 October 2026, Oraculum Digital Labs Ltd does not deploy optional analytics, advertising, profiling or social-media tracking scripts on the corporate website. A separate ORACULUM mobile sign-in verification page uses Cloudflare Turnstile for essential anti-abuse checks. In particular, the current website source does not include Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or a newsletter tracking tool.

We do not display a cookie-consent banner for optional tracking because we do not currently deploy it. Strictly necessary security technologies may be used for the mobile account protection flow. If this changes, we will update this policy and obtain any legally required consent before activating non-essential technologies.

2. Essential infrastructure and technical logs

The website is hosted on Render. Hosting and network infrastructure may use strictly necessary mechanisms to deliver and protect a website. Cloudflare Turnstile on the mobile verification page may use challenge-related technical storage or cookies where necessary to distinguish visitors from automated requests. Turnstile pre-clearance is not enabled by default. This policy does not mean that every service a visitor reaches from the site is cookie-free.

For further details, see Render's privacy notice, Cloudflare's cookie information and Cloudflare's privacy policy. Our Privacy Policy provides information about website-related personal data.

3. External websites and email

Links to external websites, app stores and email applications may open third-party services that have their own cookie and privacy practices. Their cookies are not set by this website merely because a link is displayed here.

4. Browser controls

You can inspect, delete or block cookies using your browser's privacy settings. Blocking essential cookies on other websites may affect how those websites work.

5. Contact and changes

For questions about this policy or the technologies used on this site, contact office@oraculumlabs.eu. We will revise the policy if new cookies or tracking tools are added.
