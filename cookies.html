Cookie Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains the use of cookies and similar technologies on the public corporate website at oraculumlabs.eu.

1. Current cookie use

As of 9 October 2026, Oraculum Digital Labs Ltd does not intentionally set first-party cookies or deploy optional analytics, advertising, profiling or social-media tracking scripts on this website. In particular, the current website source does not include Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or a newsletter tracking tool.

We do not display a cookie-consent banner because we do not currently deploy optional cookies or similar tracking technologies requiring consent. If this changes, we will update this policy and obtain any legally required consent before activating non-essential technologies.

2. Essential infrastructure and technical logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Hosting and network infrastructure may use strictly necessary technical mechanisms to deliver or protect a website. Separately from cookies, GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. This policy does not mean that every service a visitor reaches from the site is cookie-free.

For information on GitHub's practices, see its Privacy Statement and Cookie information. Our Privacy Policy provides information about website-related personal data.

3. External websites and email

Links to external websites, app stores and email applications may open third-party services that have their own cookie and privacy practices. Their cookies are not set by this website merely because a link is displayed here.

4. Browser controls

You can inspect, delete or block cookies using your browser's privacy settings. Blocking essential cookies on other websites may affect how those websites work.

5. Contact and changes

For questions about this policy or the technologies used on this site, contact office@oraculumlabs.eu. We will revise the policy if new cookies or tracking tools are added.
