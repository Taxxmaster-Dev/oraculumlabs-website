Request deletion of your ORACULUM account

This page applies to the ORACULUM mobile app published by Oraculum Digital Labs Ltd (Cyprus company HE 498123). It is available without signing in or installing the app.

Option 1: Delete within the app

If you can access your account, open the app's Privacy & My Data area and use its account-deletion action. Export anything you wish to keep before confirming deletion. If an error occurs, contact support; do not assume deletion completed.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have access, email office@oraculumlabs.eu with the subject ORACULUM account deletion request. Provide the email linked to your account if available, or explain that your account was anonymous. We may request proportionate verification that you control the account. Do not email passwords, login codes, payment-card details or intimate journal content.

What is deleted and what may remain?

The account-deletion process removes active account, profile and saved journey records, including related app content, derived records, subscription entitlements and notification associations, and requests removal of the linked authentication identity. Uninstalling the app is not sufficient. Limited security logs, deletion evidence, backups and records needed for legal compliance or legal claims may remain for the applicable retention periods. Data already processed by outside providers follows their respective deletion cycles. See the ORACULUM privacy notice for details.

Subscriptions are separate

Deleting the ORACULUM account does not automatically cancel a subscription billed by Apple or Google Play. Cancel it in the respective store account; we do not receive complete payment-card details.

Contact and response time
