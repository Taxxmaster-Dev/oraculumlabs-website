This notice explains how Oraculum Digital Labs Ltd handles personal information in connection with the public company website at oraculumlabs.eu. Our mobile applications, including BibleMind and ORACULUM, may have separate privacy notices covering their own features and processing.

The controller for the processing described in this notice is Oraculum Digital Labs Ltd, company registration number HE 498123, registered in Cyprus. Registered office: Andrea Araouzou 2, Stefano Plaza, 1st Floor, Office 101, Kato Polemidia 4150, Limassol, Cyprus. For any privacy-related request, email office@oraculumlabs.eu.

2. Personal data and sources

  • Communications: if you email us, we receive your email address and any name, company details, messages or attachments you choose to share.
  • Website access and security data: the hosting infrastructure may process your IP address, date and time of requests, requested pages, device/browser information and security or error logs. GitHub states that GitHub Pages logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Responding to enquiries and managing business correspondence: our legitimate interest in communicating with people who contact us (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR); where your request concerns steps before entering into a contract, Article 6(1)(b) may apply.
  • Operating and securing the website: our legitimate interest in ensuring a functional, reliable and secure website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub may also process certain hosting data for its own purposes under its privacy statement.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations: where we must keep or disclose information to comply with the law (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

4. Recipients and hosting

Information may be accessible to authorised company personnel and service providers supporting our business email, website hosting and technical administration, to the extent necessary. The website is published via GitHub Pages. See GitHub Pages information about visitor data and the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not sell visitor contact information.

5. International transfers

Some service providers may process information outside the European Economic Area. Where a transfer is subject to the GDPR, the applicable transfer rules and safeguards must be observed. You may contact us for information about the safeguards relevant to processing we control. GitHub explains its own handling and transfers in its privacy statement.

6. Retention

We keep correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to handle your request and related follow-up. We may keep relevant records longer where necessary for a continuing business relationship, applicable legal obligations, or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. Technical hosting logs are managed according to the hosting provider’s applicable practices and retention rules. You can ask us for further information about retention of data we control.

7. Your GDPR rights

Subject to the conditions and exceptions in applicable law, you may request access to, rectification or erasure of your personal data, restriction of processing, and data portability where applicable. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing. Contact us at office@oraculumlabs.eu to exercise your rights.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection (Cyprus) or another competent EU/EEA supervisory authority.

8. Cookies and automated decisions

We do not currently deploy website analytics, advertising pixels or other optional tracking scripts in the published website source. See our Cookie Policy for details. This website does not make decisions about individuals based solely on automated processing.

9. Updates

We may update this notice if the website, service providers or legal requirements change. The date shown above indicates the most recent published update.
